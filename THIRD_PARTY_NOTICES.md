# Third-party dependencies

The unmodified official Raw Accel 1.7.1 archive is bundled in vendor/rawaccel with its upstream MIT license. Interception binaries are not bundled.

- Raw Accel: https://github.com/RawAccelOfficial/rawaccel — MIT, copyright 2020 a1xd. The bridge uses its wrapper API. Retain the upstream notices with distributed runtime files.
- Newtonsoft.Json: https://github.com/JamesNK/Newtonsoft.Json — MIT, copyright James Newton-King. Retain its upstream license.
- Optional Interception: https://github.com/oblitum/Interception — separate upstream licensing, including LGPL terms and additional distribution/licensing options. Obtain and review the applicable license directly. It is not relicensed by this project's MIT license.
- .NET Framework and Windows are external platform requirements.
